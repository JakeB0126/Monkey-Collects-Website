# Frontend Direction

## Brand Direction

Baby Monkey Collects should feel:
- cozy
- jungle-inspired
- collector-focused
- premium but playful
- warm and trustworthy

Avoid:
- generic ecommerce styling
- cluttered layouts
- overly childish visuals
- esports/gamer aesthetics
- AI-looking over-detailed art

## Visual Style

Use:
- warm tan backgrounds
- rich emerald green header/accents
- cream product cards
- dark brown readable text
- subtle gold/warm accents
- rounded friendly shapes
- clean spacing

Mascot art should support the brand, not overwhelm the page.

## UI Priorities

Prioritize:
- mobile-friendly layouts
- clear navigation
- readable product cards
- strong hero section
- clean product detail pages
- easy cart flow
- clear empty/error states
- subtle hover/microinteractions

## Near-Term Frontend Work

- search
